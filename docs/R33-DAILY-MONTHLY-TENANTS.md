# R33 Daily + Monthly Tenant Billing

- New Tenant flow now starts with Monthly Resident vs Daily Stay Guest.
- Monthly tenants keep monthly rent + due day reminders.
- Daily guests use per-day rate with optional planned checkout date.
- Residents page can filter All Plans / Monthly / Daily Stay.
- Resident profile shows plan-aware rate and billing details.
- Payments page has a Daily Guests running-bill panel and separate Monthly Rent Reminders.
- Daily expected amount is calculated from active stay days × daily rate, minus rent payments recorded since the current stay began.
- Existing residents without a billingCycle remain Monthly for backward compatibility.
